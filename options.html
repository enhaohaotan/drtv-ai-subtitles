Data and cache

Privacy policy

When translation is enabled, the current batch, loaded dialogue before and after it, the programme title and nearby continuity notes are sent to OpenAI. Only subtitles near the current playback position are translated ahead. Your language choice is remembered. Translations are cached on this device for up to 30 days, within a size limit, so you can return without retranslating cached subtitles.

Nearby subtitles may be translated while playback is paused. Disabling translation stops new requests and restores DRTV subtitles. Requests already sent may incur charges.
